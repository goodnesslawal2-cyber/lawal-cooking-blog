School Jollof Rice Recipe

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Maggi (anyone of your choice)
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Satchet Tomatoes
  • Vegetable oil / Palm Oil

Instructions

  1. Boil your Rice
  2. Within 12-15 minutes, sieve the rice and parboil it for about 10-15 minutes
  3. Once your rice is parboiled, sieve it again and keep aside
  4. On your gas to the lowest and put your pot on the fire
  5. Pour a little groundnut oil in the pot, add your satchet tomatoes, maggi (to taste), salt, and pepper
  6. Stir well for about a minute, then add water and stir
  7. Then put in your rice and stir
  8. Leave your rice to steam for about 5-7 minutes
  9. Add any protein of your choice
  10. Your rice is ready
